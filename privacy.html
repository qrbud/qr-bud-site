Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: QR-bud collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server. Everything you enter or scan stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores on your device

  • History. Codes you save, share, copy or scan are kept in a list on your iPhone so you can find them again. You can delete single entries or clear the list at any time.
  • Wi-Fi passwords. If a history entry contains a Wi-Fi password, it is stored in the iOS Keychain, not in the history file. Deleting the entry deletes the password.
  • Settings. Your theme choice, whether you have seen the intro, and your print sheet options (size, paper, cut lines).

This data never leaves your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup includes app data according to your own backup settings. We have no access to it.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used only to scan QR codes live. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Photos (add only). Used only to save the QR codes you create. The app cannot read your photo library.
  • Choosing a photo to scan. When you pick a picture to read a QR code from, iOS hands the app only that picture. It is read on your iPhone and not stored.
  • Contacts and calendar. When you add a scanned contact or event, iOS shows its own screen and saves the entry itself. The app gets no access to your contacts or calendar.
  • Wi-Fi. When you join a scanned network, iOS asks you first and connects. The app sends nothing anywhere.
  • Printing. A print sheet goes only to the printer you pick in the iOS print dialog, or into a PDF you share yourself. The app finds no printers on its own.

Links and scanned content

The app shows what a scanned code contains before anything opens. If you tap "Open link", "Write email", "Call" or "Send message", the matching app on your iPhone takes over and its own privacy rules apply.

Feedback

"Send feedback" in Settings opens a draft in your mail app. Below your message it lists the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language, so we can reproduce problems. You can read and delete these lines before sending. We only get what you send, and we use it only to answer you and improve the app.

Third parties

QR-bud uses no third-party services or SDKs. If you have chosen to share analytics and crash data with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. They contain no content from the app.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
